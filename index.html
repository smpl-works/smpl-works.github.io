02 / The idea

Less to look at.

SMPL Works is a personal collection of applications I build, use, and make my own.

The idea is simple: tools should help you do your work, then get out of the way. Fewer messages. Less visual clutter. A little more room for your eyes and your mind to rest.

Only what helps. Nothing that gets in the way.
